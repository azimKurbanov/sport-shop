Sizes

Glove size

Boxing glove weight is measured in ounces (oz). Find the right size for your body weight.

  • For training and bag work — lighter gloves
  • For sparring — 14–16 oz
  • Hand wraps add extra protection
Boxing glove sizes
Your weightGlovesBest for
up to 45 kg8 ozKids and teens
45–60 kg10 ozLightweight, training
60–75 kg12 ozMiddleweight
75–90 kg14 ozHeavyweight, sparring
over 90 kg16 ozSparring and heavy bag

Training tips

  1. Warm up for 10 minutes before every session
  2. Always wrap your hands under the gloves
  3. Wear headgear and a mouthguard in sparring
  4. Stretch after every workout
